--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Patient Terms warn AI outputs may be incomplete/inaccurate and must be reviewed
by a qualified healthcare professional. No consistent UI disclaimer exists.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In scope: one reusable disclaimer component placed at every AI-output surface above.
Out of scope (defer): backend AI metadata and a clinician "reviewed" acknowledgement
table — noted below as optional follow-up, do NOT build unless asked.
This is a frontend-only ticket.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPLEMENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Create src/components/AIOutputDisclaimer.svelte:
   - Small, muted, non-dismissible inline note (match existing Tailwind styling in the
     surrounding components). Optional prop `compact` for tight card footers.
   - Copy (final wording pending legal sign-off — see open decisions):
     "AI-generated information may be incomplete or inaccurate. It does not replace
      medical advice and should be reviewed by an appropriately qualified healthcare
      professional before being relied upon."
2. Import and place it directly beneath the AI text in each file listed in CONTEXT.
   For modals, place inside the scroll area under the content, not in the footer buttons.
   For the risk-assessment routes, place once beneath the AI interpretation/summary block.
3. Do not change any AI generation, fetching, or existing layout logic.

Grep guard: after editing, `grep -rL "AIOutputDisclaimer"` across the CONTEXT file list
must return nothing (every listed surface imports it).

- No change to existing AI output behaviour, fetching, or streaming.
